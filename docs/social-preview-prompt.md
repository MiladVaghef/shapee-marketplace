## Final prompt

Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` to the deployed public HTTPS origin (including on Vercel) so social crawlers receive absolute image URLs. If it is unset, the local build resolves these image URLs against localhost, which external messaging services cannot fetch.

Use case: ads-marketing.
Create ONE polished Open Graph social link preview cover for a Persian marketplace web project, landscape aspect ratio 1.91:1, ideally 1200×630 or a proportional higher resolution. This is a finished share card, not a screenshot of a poster on a wall.
References: image 1 is the actual logo; preserve its recognizable black Persian wordmark "شاپی" with purple folded ribbon symbol. Image 2 is a website reference for its visual identity: cheerful premium marketplace, white space, violet #792edf and lavender, rounded product cards and tiny warm golden accents. Do not copy the dated screenshot's unrelated logos or tiny UI text.
Composition: luxurious bright pearl-white/lavender background, generous safe margins at least 8%; upper right brand logo faithfully based on the reference, medium-large. Large clear Persian headline on the right half: "همه چیز، یک‌جا". Beneath in smaller elegant Persian type: "بازار آنلاین محصولات و کسب‌وکارها". Restrained tiny English footer "SHOPPIE • MARKETPLACE" at bottom right, with wide tracking and dark muted purple.
Left half and lower center: a beautiful art-directed dimensional collage of a floating desktop storefront panel and a smaller upright mobile storefront panel, both white softly rounded, showing plausible clean RTL product cards with premium brown leather sneaker, matte-black headphones, graphite smartwatch, and a minimal bright villa photograph. The miniature interfaces should have simple purple accents and product photos with visual hierarchy, not random paragraphs or unreadable dense text. Add one small violet shopping bag with the ribbon symbol and one small soft-gold accent as subtle foreground depth. Keep the focus balanced between branding/message and the product UI. No heavy gadget frames, no desktop stand, no browser brand chrome. Interface panels should look like this project's light theme. Soft realistic ambient shadows, excellent material rendering, crisp editorial typography, sophisticated clean web-design portfolio cover.
Keep it modern, cheerful, warm, vibrant and alive without clutter. Primary purple has a rich saturated tone; background remains mostly white. Flat-on overall composition with gently angled floating panels for depth. Avoid dark backgrounds, excessive neon glows, gradients over text, arbitrary trust badges, fake metrics, watermarks, social logos, unnecessary slogans or extra text. Make it legible and recognizable at thumbnail sizes. All important content fits within a centered 1200×630 safe rectangle.
